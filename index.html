    01

    Who we are

    Martin's Mocktails is a wellness mocktail bar and café that serves fresh juices, mocktails, smoothies, bowls and bites, with drinks named after the songs we love. In this policy, "Martin's", "we", "us" and "our" mean Oispic International, the business that runs the Martin's Mocktails café, website and app.

    Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we are the data fiduciary for the personal data described here. That means we decide why and how it is used, and we are responsible for looking after it.

    02

    What this covers

    This policy applies whenever you deal with us through:

    • Our website, including the menu, the mood quiz and the order list you can build there.
    • The Martin's Mocktails app on Android, iOS and the web, including ordering, the wallet, gifts, group orders, the Club, plans and rewards.
    • Our café, when you order at the counter using your phone number, enquire about a party counter, or book an event with us.
    • Ask Martin's, the in-app assistant that answers questions about our menu.

    It does not cover other websites or apps we link to, such as Instagram or WhatsApp. They have their own privacy policies.

    03

    What we collect

    We collect information in three ways: what you give us, what is created when you use our services, and a small amount that comes from the partners who help us run them.

    Things you give us

    Account details

    Your mobile number (used to sign in with a one-time code), your name, and, if you choose, your birthday (day and month only) so we can send you a birthday treat.

    Needed for the app

    Taste preferences

    Optional choices that personalise the menu: your wellness goal, ingredients you'd rather avoid, your favourite artists and your usual ice level.

    Optional

    Orders and requests

    What you order, sizes, notes for the kitchen, group orders you start or join, and party counter or event enquiries (name, phone, date, number of guests and your message).

    Needed to serve you

    Gifts and referrals

    If you send wallet credit as a gift or invite a friend, the phone number you send it to, and the referral code you used when you joined.

    Only if you use them

    Things created when you use our services

    Order and wallet history

    Your past orders, wallet top-ups, spends, refunds, bonuses, gifts and rewards, Club membership and plan status, and the totals that decide which rewards you've earned.

    Payment records

    The amount, date, status and reference number of each payment. Your card, UPI or bank details are handled by our payment partner and never reach our servers.

    Device information

    A notification token and your device type (Android, iOS or web), so we can tell you when your order is ready. We also keep basic technical logs (such as time of request and errors) to keep the service secure and working.

    Questions to Ask Martin's

    The question you type. We send the assistant only your question and our menu, never your name, phone number, birthday or order history.

    Things we don't collect

    • We don't ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
    • We don't collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank login.
    • The website's mood quiz and order list run inside your own browser. Your answers and the drinks you pick are not sent to us unless you choose to share the list with us, for example by sending it on WhatsApp.
    • We don't knowingly collect health records. Menu descriptions such as "immunity boost" describe ingredients; they are not based on information about you.
    !

    About the "ingredients to avoid" setting. If you list something you avoid (for example, a fruit you're allergic to), we use it only to filter and flag menu items for you. It isn't shared with anyone. Please still tell our staff about allergies when you order, as our kitchen handles many fruits, nuts and dairy.

    06

    Where it's stored

    Our servers and database are located in India. Some of our partners (such as Google for notifications and fonts, and the AI provider for Ask Martin's) may process limited data outside India. When that happens, we only send what's needed for that task, and we follow any restrictions the Government of India sets on transfers abroad.

    07

    How long we keep it

    We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires.

    DataHow long
    Sign-in codesA few minutes, until they expire. We store them only in scrambled (hashed) form.
    Your account, preferences and walletWhile your account is open. When you delete it, we remove your profile within 30 days.
    Bills, orders and payment recordsFor as long as GST and tax law requires. After you delete your account, we keep these with your name and phone number removed.

    BackupsOverwritten on a rolling basis, so deleted data leaves our backups shortly after.

    Any money left in your wallet when you close your account is handled under our Wallet & refund terms link. Please use or request a refund of your balance before you delete your account.

    08

    How we protect it

    • All connections to our website, app and servers use HTTPS encryption.
    • Our database is encrypted at rest, sits on a private network that only our app servers can reach, and is backed up every day.
    • Sign-in codes and staff PINs are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
    • Our staff see only what their role needs, and every action in our admin system is logged.
    • We check every request our app receives to block misuse.

    No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires, explain what happened, and tell you what we are doing about it.

    09

    Your rights

    Under the DPDP Act you have these rights. Using them is free.

    A

    Know & access

    Ask what data we hold about you, how we use it, and who we've shared it with.

    B

    Correct & update

    Fix anything that's wrong or out of date. Most details you can edit yourself in the app.

    C

    Erase

    Delete your account and personal data, except records the law makes us keep.

    D

    Withdraw consent

    Change your mind at any time, for example by switching off offers or optional settings.

    E

    Complain

    Raise a complaint with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

    F

    Nominate

    Name someone to use these rights for you if you're unable to, through death or incapacity.

    How to use them

    1. In the app: go to Profile to edit your details, change notification settings, or choose Delete account.
    2. By email or phone: contact us using the details in Contact us. We may ask you to confirm a one-time code sent to your registered mobile number, so we know it's really you.
    3. We reply within 30 days, and tell you if we need more time and why.

    Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it. If you withdraw consent that's needed for a service (for example, your phone number for sign-in), we won't be able to keep providing that service.

    10

    Children

    Our app accounts are for people aged 18 and over. Our kids' menu (Twinkle Toes) is meant to be ordered by a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, and we don't track children's behaviour or send them targeted offers.

    If you think a child has given us their data without permission, please contact us and we will delete it.

    11

    Cookies & storage

    Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It may save a few small things in your own browser so the site works the way you left it, such as the drinks in your order list or whether you turned the café music on. This stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

    The app stores your sign-in session securely on your phone so you don't have to sign in every time. Signing out removes it.

    12

    Messages & offers

    We send two kinds of messages:

    • Service messages, such as your sign-in code, payment confirmations and "your order is ready". These are part of the service.
    • Offers and news, such as new drinks, events and rewards. You can turn these off in the app's settings or your phone's notification settings at any time.
    13

    New features

    We're always working on new things. If we add a feature that needs new kinds of personal data, such as health or activity data from a wearable device, we will update this policy first and ask for your clear, separate consent before collecting anything. Such features will always be optional.

    14

    Changes

    We may update this policy from time to time. We'll change the "Last updated" date at the top, and if a change is significant we'll tell you in the app or by message before it takes effect. Where a change needs your consent, we'll ask for it.
